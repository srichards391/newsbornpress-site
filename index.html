Questions

Before you order

How long does it take?

You'll get a proof within 3 to 5 business days of sending the questionnaire. One round of edits is included, and the final files follow. Printed editions ship within a few days of your OK on the proof.

What do I actually receive?

Every order gets the digital files: the 11×17 broadsheet, the 8½×11 letter edition, classic grey versions of both, and five phone cards. Printed orders also get one or two 11×17 prints on heavy cardstock, shipped free in the US.

Can you print it for me?

Yes. Choose the Single print or the Grandparent Edition and we print it in Erie and mail it to you. Printing it yourself? The 11×17 looks best at a local print or copy shop on matte paper, and the letter edition prints fine at home.

Can I order before the baby arrives?

Yes, and it makes a good shower gift. Order anytime and fill in the questionnaire once the baby is here.

Where do the facts come from?

Public sources for the exact date: the National Weather Service, market data, government price reports, the music charts and the sports pages. We keep a list of sources for every paper.

What happens to our photos and details?

We use them only to make your paper and delete them 30 days after delivery. We never show your baby in our marketing without your written permission.

Do you use AI?

We use AI tools to help research the day's facts and draft copy. A person reads and checks every word before it reaches you.

About the press

Started at a kitchen table in Erie

Newsborn Press started at a kitchen table in Erie, Pennsylvania, in the fall of 2026. A new dad made his daughter, Roxanne May, a newspaper for the day she was born, with the weather off the lake, the price of gas, the song on the radio and a letter from her parents. The family kept asking for copies.

So now we make them for other families. Every Birth Edition is written for one baby and checked by hand.

Steve Richards, Founder & Pressman
