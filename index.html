About the press

Started at a kitchen table in Erie

Newsborn Press started at a kitchen table in Erie, Pennsylvania, in the fall of 2026. A new dad made his daughter a newspaper for the day she was born, with the weather off the lake, the price of gas, the song on the radio and a letter from her parents. The family kept asking for copies.

So now we make them for other families. Every Birth Edition is written for one baby and checked by hand.

Steve Richards, Founder & Pressman
