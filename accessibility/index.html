Accessibility

Every family should be able to order a paper and read this site, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Last reviewed October 8, 2026

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA on newsbornpress.com.

What we've done

  • Tested every page with an automated accessibility checker (axe) in light and dark mode, and fixed what it found.
  • Checked that the whole site works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Written text descriptions for every image, including the sample newspaper pages.
  • Labeled every form field and announced form results to screen readers.
  • Kept text contrast readable in both light and dark mode.
  • Turned off motion for anyone whose device is set to reduce motion.
  • Not used an accessibility overlay or widget. We'd rather fix the site itself.

What isn't perfect yet

  • The paper itself is a designed print layout delivered as a PDF, and the sample images use small newspaper type. If you'd like your paper in large print or as a plain-text version, ask and we'll make one at no charge.
  • Checkout happens on Etsy, which runs its own pages. If something there gets in your way, email us and we'll take your order directly.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, email hello@newsbornpress.com with the page and what happened. We'll reply within two business days and help you place your order another way in the meantime.
