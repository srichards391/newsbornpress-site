For the new grandparents

A gift for new grandparents that goes on the wall, not in a drawer: their grandchild on the front page of a real newspaper, with the news from the day the baby was born.

The Grandparent Edition · $80 · Free US shipping

What they get

  • Two identical 11×17 prints on heavy cardstock, one for the parents and one for the grandparents, rolled in a sturdy tube.
  • The baby on the front page: their name in the nameplate, their photo under the banner, and the time, weight and length if the parents share them.
  • The world they were born into: the weather, the prices, the No. 1 song and the day's news, researched and sourced for the birth date.
  • A family masthead. Grandpa as Editor in Chief, Grandma as Managing Editor, the cat as Staff Mascot. The parents decide who gets which title.
  • The digital files too: the 11×17 broadsheet, a letter-size edition, grey newsprint versions of both and five phone cards for the family group chat.

How gifting works

  • Order the Printed Edition on Etsy and choose the Grandparent Edition.
  • Put the parents' email in the order form. We ask them for the name, date, photo and a few lines about the day, so you don't need any details.
  • Baby not born yet? That works. Order now and we start the paper when the questionnaire comes back.
  • The parents see a proof first, with one round of changes included. Prints ship in 5 to 7 business days after they approve it.

Questions

What's a good gift for new grandparents?
A front page about their grandchild. The Grandparent Edition from Newsborn Press is two identical 11×17 newspaper prints about the new baby, one for the parents and one for the grandparents, with the baby's name in the nameplate, their photo, their birth stats and the real news, weather and prices from the day they were born. It's $80 with free US shipping, and the digital files come with it.

Can I order it before the baby is born?
Yes. Order any time and put the parents' email in the Etsy order form. We start the paper when the questionnaire comes back after the baby arrives.

Do I need to know the birth details?
No. We ask the parents directly for the name, date, photo and the story of the day, so the gift can stay a surprise until it's done.

Does it ship outside the US?
Prints ship to US addresses only for now. For family abroad, order the Digital Edition ($35) and print it locally on A3 or A4.
