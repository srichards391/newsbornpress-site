Privacy

Short version: this website sets no cookies and doesn't track you. We use your baby's details only to make your paper, and we delete them 30 days after delivery.

Effective October 8, 2026

Who we are

Newsborn Press is a small family business in Erie, Pennsylvania, run by Steve Richards. You can reach us any time at hello@newsbornpress.com.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking pixels.
  • Our fonts and images are stored on this site, so your browser doesn't contact Google or anyone else to show them.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, to keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • If we ever add site statistics or cookies, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

What we collect

  • The due-date list. The signup form opens an email to us. If you send it, we get your email address and, if you include it, your due date. We use it to send one reminder, and we'll remove you whenever you ask.
  • Orders. To make your paper we need your name and email, your baby's name, birth date and town, your answers to our questionnaire, and the photo you send. For a printed paper we also need a shipping address.
  • Email. Anything you send to hello@newsbornpress.com. Our email is hosted by Fastmail.

How we use it

  • Only to make, deliver and support your order, and to answer you.
  • Orders placed on Etsy are also covered by Etsy's privacy policy. Etsy handles payment; we never see your card.
  • Printed papers are printed from the finished file by a local Erie print shop and mailed to the address on your order.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described here.

Your baby's photo and story

We never use your baby's photo, name or story in our marketing, on social media or as a sample unless the parents give us written permission. Permission from a gift-giver doesn't count.

How long we keep it

Your photo, answers and finished files are kept in a private folder and deleted 30 days after delivery. Emails and Etsy order records may be kept longer for our business and tax records.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us. We'll reply within a few business days.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: we don't track visitors, so there is nothing for these signals to turn off. We honor them anyway.

Children

Our papers are about babies, but everything we receive comes from their parents or other adults. We don't knowingly collect information online from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
